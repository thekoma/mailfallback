# PM critique

*Voice: 10y PM, optimising for "shippable v1.0 with minimum future regret". I am here to draw lines, not to admire the problem.*

---

## Bottom line up front

- **The single highest-leverage change is renaming "backup" in user-facing strings *before* the restic feature ships to production.** Every day the current vocabulary stays live is a day the support burden grows linearly. Cost is hours of label edits; benefit is a forever-cleaner mental model. Do this first, do it this week.
- **Cheapest big win: rewrite the empty state on `/accounts` and the first-run dashboard copy.** Two paragraphs of copy reach 100% of new users. Highest ROI text in the app.
- **Do NOT rename DB tables, models, or routes in v1.0.** The 4-stage lexicon needs to win in the GUI first. We can ship a 90% improvement without a single Alembic migration.
- **The "chain visualisation" is the second-largest leverage point but it is a *design*, not a *fix*.** It earns a milestone of its own. Don't let it blur into the lexicon work.
- **Compliance reporting for P3 (Giulia) is a v1.1+ concern.** It is genuinely valuable but it is *not* on the critical path to credibility for the existing audience (P1, P2, P4, P5).

---

## Scope discipline (what NOT to do today)

1. **Don't rename `BackupDestination` / `AccountBackup` models.** Tempting because they leak the wrong word into the API surface, but the cost (Alembic migration, FK rename, test churn, doc sweep) is ~XL and the user-visible payoff is zero. Wrap them with API-layer aliases if needed.
2. **Don't build the per-org "evidence" / report pane for Giulia (P3).** Real audience for v1.0 is P1/P2/P4/P5. Compliance reporting is a credibility tax we don't have to pay yet.
3. **Don't redesign the `/restore` page to merge IMAP-restore with snapshot-restore.** Two flows, one word — yes, that's a problem, but the fix is *naming* and *signposting*, not unification. Unification is M3 territory.
4. **Don't ship dark mode this round.** It's spec'd but unrelated. Adding it now muddies the diff, dilutes review, doubles the visual QA surface.
5. **Don't add new admin pages.** The temptation will be "let's add /admin/chain-health" or "/admin/reports". Resist. Surface it on existing pages first; promote to a page only when telemetry says people are looking for it.
6. **Don't move "Stores" out of the admin section.** Every UX critic will want to "expose stores to users earlier". Ignore them — exposing storage paths to non-admin users is a footgun that isn't worth the complexity.
7. **Don't translate the lexicon into Italian beyond the strings the IT-speaking maintainers already touch.** Pick EN as canonical, ship IT as a *parallel* exercise after EN converges. Two moving targets = neither shipping.
8. **Don't rewrite the audit log into a "compliance evidence" surface.** It's a developer/admin debug tool today. Keep it there. Compliance evidence is a different artefact entirely.

## v1.0 cutline

**Ship now (v1.0):** items 1–9. That is, *the lexicon polish* + *the offsite feature de-burying* + *flash-message disambiguation*. This is roughly 3–5 days of work for one engineer with design support, and it removes the largest source of confusion the product has today.

**Defer (v1.1, next quarter):** items 10–13. Chain visualisation, first-run checklist, admin overview, restore wizard. These deserve their own design pass, not a tail-end of the lexicon PR.

**Defer further (v1.2+):** items 14–15. Model renames, compliance reporting.

The litmus test for the cutline: *can a new user get from `docker compose up` to "I can see my mail and I know where my offsite snapshots live" in one hour, using only the words in the UI?* Items 1–9 get us there. Items 10+ make it elegant; they don't make it possible.

## Roadmap shape

**M1 — Lexicon polish (v1.0, ~1 week).**
Scope: items 1–9. Output: every user-facing string aligned to the chosen lexicon, offsite feature de-buried, flash messages disambiguated, "Destination" collision resolved in `/restore`. Ship behind a release-note toast. **Entry criterion:** Phase-5 lexicon decision made by Andrea. **Exit criterion:** zero "backup" occurrences denoting the local mirror in templates; admin nav renamed; QA pass by P2-style tester.

**M2 — Chain visualisation (v1.1, ~3 weeks).**
Scope: items 7, 10, 11. Output: account-detail page leads with a 4-stage chain strip; dashboard gains a chain-health tile; admin gets a chain-health overview table; first-run guided checklist on empty dashboard. **Entry criterion:** M1 has shipped and the lexicon hasn't drifted under load. **Exit criterion:** any P5 user can describe the four stages out loud after one minute on the dashboard.

**M3 — Compliance / reporting / restore wizard (v1.2, ~6 weeks).**
Scope: items 13, 15, plus a per-account "evidence card" suitable for screenshotting into a board pack; "I lost my disk" guided restore; CSV/PDF export of retention policy + last-snapshot dates per account. Adds the P3 audience without compromising P1/P2/P4 daily UX. **Entry criterion:** at least one P3-style user (not Andrea) has installed MFB and given feedback. **Exit criterion:** Giulia can answer "is our mail safe?" with a one-click export.

The key roadmap discipline: each milestone has a *single audience promotion* — M1 fixes existing users' confusion, M2 wins P5/P4 onboarding, M3 unlocks P3 as a viable audience.

## Things I disagree with in the current-state doc

1. **The "honesty audit" (worst offenders table) treats all 6 issues as equivalent.** They aren't. "Last sync: X ago" being last-attempt rather than last-success is genuinely misleading and a bug. "Backup configured" being ambiguous is a *naming* problem solved by M1. Lumping them flattens priorities. PM call: split into "naming will fix this" vs "we owe the user a code change".

2. **The doc treats the "buried offsite feature" as a vocabulary problem.** It's not. Even if we called it "Cloud Snapshot Vault" with sparkles, hiding it under a `<details>` block on page 2 is an *IA* problem. The lexicon work won't fix it; surface promotion will (item #3 in my stack). Don't conflate the two.

3. **The data-model section recommends MED-cost route renames.** I'd push back: route renames break bookmarks, mkdocs anchors, and any third-party scripts. Cost is HIGH, not MED, when you count the user-trust impact. Wrap with redirects if we ever do it; don't enthuse about it as a tier-2 option.

4. **The doc undersells what's working.** The hero panel, the live HTMX sync state, the system-status sticky bar — these are competitive advantages over almost every self-hosted alternative. The "what's working" section should be twice as long. PM voice: *we are closer to v1.0 than the audit makes it sound.*

## Decisions I want from the founder

1. **Q: Do we rename DB tables / models in v1.0, or only labels?**
   *Recommendation: labels only.* Models stay `BackupDestination` and `AccountBackup`. Document the desired future rename so v1.2 can do it cleanly. Reasoning: rename cost is paid in Alembic, FK constraints, and test churn for zero user-visible benefit *if* the labels are right.

2. **Q: Pick one of the three Phase-5 lexicon proposals at the 13:00 review, or take more time?**
   *Recommendation: pick one today, hard.* Reasoning: time-box. The cost of "the wrong one for two weeks" is small; the cost of "no decision for two weeks" is the audit losing momentum and never landing in code.

3. **Q: Italian or English as the canonical lexicon?**
   *Recommendation: English canonical, Italian as a parallel rollout.* Reasoning: the codebase, models, env vars, and docs site are EN. Two canonicals is two failure modes.

4. **Q: Do we ship the chain visualisation diagram in v1.0 or hold for M2?**
   *Recommendation: hold for M2.* Reasoning: it's a design artifact, not a bugfix. Putting it in M1 doubles the M1 scope and risks scope creep killing the lexicon polish entirely.

5. **Q: Do we promote "Stores" out of the admin section as part of M1?**
   *Recommendation: no.* Reasoning: stores are infrastructure plumbing for self-hosted operators. Exposing storage paths to ordinary users is a footgun (broken paths, permission errors) and a support load increase. Re-evaluate if/when we add user-selectable storage (currently spec'd, not built).

6. **Q: Do we treat P3 (compliance) as a v1.0 audience or defer to M3?**
   *Recommendation: defer cleanly to M3.* Reasoning: chasing P3 now means building reports nobody validates. We don't have a P3 design partner today. Get one (or formally drop the persona) before investing engineering hours.
