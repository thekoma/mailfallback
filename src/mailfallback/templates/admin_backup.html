Repositories

Off-site repositories where mailbox snapshots are stored. Each Repository is an encrypted restic repository backed by S3 or a local path. Mailboxes pick a Repository in their backup policy.

Snapshots are useless without your MAILFALLBACK_SECRET_KEY and a Postgres dump. See disaster recovery for what to back up alongside your repositories.
